If something slips, breadth shrinks before the comparisons

The proposal names three ways the plan can slip, each with a response. In the first two the breadth shrinks and the two paired comparisons stay. In the third, the result itself is the finding.

A conclusion should carry the evidence to check it

If this plan holds, the thesis will leave three things. They are expected contributions, not results.

01 · Expected

A representation linking claims to evidence

A record that links every claim to its sources and runs.

02 · Expected

A protocol for independent assessment

It measures how well an outside auditor can assess a claim.

03 · Expected

A tested method for acting on audit findings

It turns audit findings into the next action, under a fixed budget.

What would it take to trust research done by AI?

The narrow answer of this talk: another researcher can check the work. The aim of the thesis is that the next AI-written paper carries enough evidence for a reader to tell.
